Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Wonder Files is a personal tool that its owner uses to publish short educational videos to his own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram).

What we access

When the owner connects an account, the tool receives an access token from the platform's official API. It is used only to upload the owner's own videos and to read the statistics of those videos (views, likes, comments, shares), so the tool can learn which videos work best.

What we store

Access tokens and video statistics are stored privately on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect personal data about viewers or any other users.

Removal

The owner can disconnect the tool at any time from the platform's settings; the stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/B0Gd4n28/wonder-files.
